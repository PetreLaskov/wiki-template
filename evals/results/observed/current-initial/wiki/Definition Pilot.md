### S1

For a short glossary-learning session, twelve volunteers tried Recall Cards first and Case Notebook second with the same prompt set; assignment and order were not randomized. Across 60 exact-definition prompts, both methods produced 54 correct answers. Reported session times were 4 minutes for Cards and 8 minutes for Notebook.
