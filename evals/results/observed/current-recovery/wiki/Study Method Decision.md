## Recommendation

Provisionally retain Case Notebook for unfamiliar-case work. Do not replace it with Cards on a speed rationale or add Cards by default as a time-saving extra: the corrected session totals favor Notebook in that session, and the unfamiliar-case observations also favor Notebook users, with causal limitations in both comparisons. [[Timing Correction#S1]] [[Novel Case Audit#S1]] Cards remain an option if a relevant comparison demonstrates a useful benefit; a combined regimen and the best allocation of study time have not been established. This is an agent proposal under the user's understanding-first, time-second priorities, not a user commitment or a universal method ranking.

## Reasoning

- **Exact-definition recall and comparable time:** Correctness remains 54/60 for each method. The original 4-minute Cards figure omitted 6 minutes of setup; Notebook's 8-minute figure included setup. Comparable totals are therefore Cards 10 minutes versus Notebook 8, so the original doubled-speed claim is withdrawn for this total-session comparison. [[Timing Correction#S1]] [[Timing Correction#S2]] Cards came first, Notebook second, with repeated prompts and no randomized assignment/order. These observations do not establish equal general efficacy or causal Notebook time savings. [[Definition Pilot#S1]] [[Definition Pilot#S2]]
- **Unfamiliar-case understanding:** The separate eight-person audit reports 16/24 correct for Cards users and 22/24 for Notebook users. This is evidence about unfamiliar applied answers, not comprehensive understanding; self-selection and uncontrolled prior knowledge/allocation leave alternative explanations. No timing was collected, so the audit cannot establish Notebook's time cost. [[Novel Case Audit#S1]] [[Novel Case Audit#S2]]
- **Inferential bridge:** Exact-definition correctness and unfamiliar-case performance measure different outcomes. Since understanding comes first, the unfamiliar-case observations support preserving case work pending a controlled comparison. The corrected total-session timing also removes the former reason to favor Cards for saving time in a short definition session. That supports the provisional allocation above, without proving Notebook superior generally. [[Timing Correction#S1]] [[Novel Case Audit#S1]]

## Strongest alternative

Cards-only could still become the strongest option for a narrowly exact-definition goal if reusable setup yields lower comparable total time across repeated sessions while accuracy is maintained. That is a hypothesis: repeated-session setup amortization was not measured, so this corpus supplies no break-even estimate or demonstrated long-run Cards advantage. [[Timing Correction#S2]] Cards could also prove competitive for unfamiliar cases if prior knowledge and selection explain the audit difference, but this remains an alternative explanation, not demonstrated parity. [[Novel Case Audit#S1]] Given the user's priorities and current evidence, neither possibility warrants replacing Notebook or adding routine duplicate study now.
