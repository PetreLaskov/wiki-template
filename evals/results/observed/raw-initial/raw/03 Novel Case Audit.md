# Lumen novel-case audit

Fictional development fixture. Origin: lumen-transfer-1. Dated 2026-08-06.
This is a separate convenience sample of eight volunteers, not a replication of the glossary pilot.

On 24 unfamiliar applied scenarios, Cards users answered 16 correctly and Notebook users answered 22 correctly.
Participants selected their method; prior knowledge and allocation were not controlled. These observations do not establish a causal method effect.
The audit did not collect timing, delayed retention, or wellbeing data.
The team recommends retaining case-based work for a further controlled pilot rather than asserting a universal winner.
