# Lumen definition pilot

Fictional development fixture. No claim in this document describes real people or research.
Origin: lumen-pilot-1. Author: Lumen internal study team. Dated 2026-08-01.

The team compared Recall Cards and a Case Notebook for a short glossary-learning session.
Twelve volunteers tried Cards first and Notebook second with the same prompt set; assignment and order were not randomized.
Across 60 exact-definition prompts, Cards produced 54 correct answers and Notebook produced 54 correct answers.
The reported session times were 4 minutes for Cards and 8 minutes for Notebook.
The pilot did not measure novel-case transfer, delayed retention, wellbeing, or performance outside this session.
The team cautioned that repeated prompts and fixed order prevent a clean causal comparison of the methods.
