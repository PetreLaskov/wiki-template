# Index

## Charter

**Purpose:** understand the fictional Lumen study-method corpus and choose study
methods with accurate understanding first, then avoiding unnecessary study time.

**Recurring questions:**

1. What do exact-definition scores establish, and do they transfer to unfamiliar cases?
2. Should Case Notebook be dropped in favour of Recall Cards?
3. Does the evidence warrant the newsletter's universal speed promise?
4. What is known about six-month retention and wellbeing?

**Source posture:** three fully read snapshots, two empirical origins. The pilot
and audit report uncontrolled observations on different tasks and samples. The
newsletter repeats the pilot and supplies no independent corroboration. Internal
team and promotional frames dominate; reported outcomes are evidence within this
fictional corpus, while recommendations remain interpretations.

**Page economics:** three short source ledgers preserve exact claims and caveats;
one analysis owns the reusable judgment. Do not create separate concept or question
pages unless future retrieval needs justify them.

**Boundaries:** no claims about real people or research, causal superiority, universal
learning efficiency, six-month retention, or wellbeing from these snapshots. User
priorities are values, not measured outcomes.

## State of the Model

Cards and Notebook tied at 54/60 exact definitions in the pilot, with reported session
times of 4 and 8 minutes; fixed order and repeated prompts limit causality.
([[Definition Pilot]] S1–S2)

Notebook users scored 22/24 versus Cards users' 16/24 on unfamiliar cases in a separate,
self-selected sample. This informs caution about replacement without proving a method
effect or contradicting the different recall outcome. ([[Novel Case Audit]] S1–S2)

**Current judgment:** retain case-based work provisionally; see [[Lumen Study Choice]].
The live interpretive tension is the newsletter's universal speed/replacement promise
versus the empirical reports' narrow scope. The newsletter adds no independent study.
([[Team Newsletter]] S1–S2)

Six-month retention and wellbeing remain unmeasured. ([[Definition Pilot]] S3;
[[Novel Case Audit]] S3) Compilation is complete for the supplied text; causal and
long-term questions remain open.
