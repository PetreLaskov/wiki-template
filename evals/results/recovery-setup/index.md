# Working understanding

## Charter

- **Purpose:** Understand the fictional Lumen evidence and choose study methods, prioritizing accurate understanding first and avoiding unnecessary study time second.
- **Recurring questions:** What distinguishes exact-definition recall from unfamiliar-case performance? Should Cards replace Notebook? Is the universal speed promise warranted? What is known about six-month retention and wellbeing?
- **Evidence posture:** Three fully read text snapshots represent two evidential origins: a small nonrandomized definition pilot plus its derivative newsletter, and a separate self-selected unfamiliar-case audit. Neither comparison establishes causal superiority.
- **Boundaries:** This fictional corpus cannot establish real-world efficacy, a universal winner, six-month retention, or wellbeing effects. Do not count the newsletter as independent corroboration.
- **Page economics:** Three compact source ledgers preserve evidence; one decision owns the interpretation and recurring answer. No separate concept or practice pages are needed.
