Run the **synthesize** mode for: $ARGUMENTS

Procedure (canonical: `AGENTS.md` § Modes; extended: `wiki/_operations.md` § 5):

1. Pull the in-scope pages (domain, hub, or explicit list from the index).
2. Distill the **through-line** — the single argument or pattern that organizes them.
   If you can't name it in one sentence, the synthesis isn't ready: surface that and stop.
3. Choose the type:
   - **synthesis** — maps territory (sub-areas + what holds them together);
   - **thesis** — argues a position from accumulated evidence.
4. Draft from `wiki/_templates.md`. Cite at claim-anchor level where anchors exist.
5. If this page should now route its domain, give it a **Decision Map**
   (task → page → guardrail) and list it as the domain's hub in the index.
6. Add Related entries on covered pages (bidirectional); index under the primary domain;
   append a `wiki/log.md` entry.

A good synthesis tells you what the field looks like seen whole. A bad one enumerates
pages — if you're listing without distilling, stop and revisit step 2.
