# Research and design judgment

Reviewed: **2026-09-15**. This is a bounded primary-source review, not a systematic literature review or a benchmark of this implementation. Links identify the material inspected; mutable repositories may change. No competing implementation was installed or independently reproduced during this review.

## Judgment

**Build a small, inspectable compiler for a revisable personal knowledge base, with direct access to original evidence and explicit correction paths.** Keep the reading and writing experience in Markdown. Use deterministic tools for bookkeeping and integrity; use agents for interpretation, inquiry, comparison, and synthesis. Increase infrastructure only after an actual workload exposes a limitation.

The premise to repair is that a prominent author's idea, a popular derivative, or more architecture implies stronger intellectual assistance. Reputation is a useful search prior. It does not establish source fidelity, better judgments, sustained usefulness, or affordable maintenance. Here, “stronger” means that important work becomes easier to understand, verify, extend, correct, and resume, at a burden the owner will actually tolerate.

The system should preserve two different assets: **what the sources say**, and **what the owner and agents have worked out from them**. A convincing synthesis is valuable, but it is not a new independent witness for its own conclusions. A source snapshot is evidence of what was captured, not a guarantee that its contents are true.

## Original idea: what survives scrutiny

[Karpathy's LLM Wiki gist](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f), created April 4, 2026, proposes immutable source material, an agent-maintained linked wiki, and an agent instruction/schema layer, with ingestion, querying, and maintenance. It explicitly presents a modular idea rather than a validated implementation. Its durable contribution is making synthesis a maintained, reusable artifact. Keep that. The gist's claims about near-zero maintenance cost and reliable agent upkeep are practitioner expectations, not measured guarantees. Treat them as hypotheses to test. The gist also recommends optional search and allows small installations to stay simple.

**Our design inference:** compilation and retrieval solve different problems. Retain compiled concepts and arguments for orientation and reuse; retrieve raw evidence when precision, challenge, novelty, or correction calls for it. A wiki-only answering policy would make its omissions and interpretation errors harder to discover.

## What to implement now

These are design judgments for this reconstruction, informed by the review rather than experimentally established results for this implementation.

1. **Keep the core portable.** Plain source files and readable Markdown should remain useful without a service, a particular model, or a particular viewer. A small machine-readable registry can support deterministic checks without turning every sentence into a database row.
2. **Give important claims recoverable evidence.** Associate a claim with a source version and an exact usable locator. Preserve enough context to inspect what supports the claim, including qualifications and exceptions. Distinguish quotation, faithful paraphrase, inference, hypothesis, and personal experience where confusing them would matter.
3. **Make corrections propagate.** Track which maintained pages depend on a source. A changed, superseded, or withdrawn source should identify affected pages for review. Do not equate a changed hash with a false claim, or an unchanged hash with a still-current claim.
4. **Keep provenance separate from popularity.** Record common origin when several sources repeat one report. Repetition should not silently become independent corroboration. Preserve incompatible accounts rather than forcing an early reconciliation.
5. **Support inquiry, not just storage.** A useful inquiry records its question, competing explanations, current position, live uncertainty, and the observation or argument that would change it. A useful decision records the actual choice, reasons, revisit condition, and later outcome. Create these when the work warrants them rather than filling every template on every ingest.
6. **Use summaries as navigation.** The agent should be able to search, inspect a page, follow references, reopen evidence, and stop with a bounded answer when the evidence remains insufficient. A fresh question must be allowed to expose what the current synthesis missed.
7. **Make work resumable and edits inspectable.** Small coherent changes, explicit unfinished work, and a compact activity record support continuity. Concurrent agents need bounded ownership of edits; prose instructions alone do not prevent lost updates.
8. **Put routine invariants in code.** Check registered paths, source fingerprints, citation targets, identifiers, and maintenance state. Leave entailment, source independence, relevance, interpretation, and usefulness to explicit semantic review. Passing structural checks must never label the knowledge true.
9. **Keep experience informative.** When a real failure occurs, retain the failed question or correction as a regression example. Change the instruction or tool that caused it, then check affected and unrelated tasks. Retire obsolete guidance.
10. **Preserve the owner's role.** Record their aims and questions without silently replacing them with what is easiest to measure. Improved understanding should appear in explanations they can use, distinctions they can apply, and choices they can revisit. Page count, citation count, and benchmark scores are proxies.

## What not to adopt by default

- A vector database, graph database, embedding service, web app, or continuous background ingestion merely to look complete.
- Automatic promotion of every answer or conversational impression into established knowledge.
- A universal ontology or mandatory exhaustive atomic-claim extraction. Apply more structure where disputes, dependencies, precision, or stakes justify its maintenance cost.
- A prohibition on reading raw sources once a summary exists.
- A single “confidence” number that conflates source reliability, source agreement, inference quality, and agent certainty.
- Automatic contradiction “resolution” by overwriting whichever account is older. Differences may concern scope, time, definition, population, or a real unresolved disagreement.
- Fixed review cadences copied from a demo; set review conditions according to the volatility and consequence of the actual claim.
- Claims that provenance, a graph, an error ledger, or a second agent guarantees truth. Each can make errors easier to find; each can also be applied superficially.

## Evaluation that can change the architecture

Start with representative work, including an exact-source question, a cross-source synthesis, a disagreement, an unsupported question, a changed source, an interrupted session, and an unfamiliar question whose answer was omitted from existing summaries. Include a source that repeats another source, so corroboration is tested rather than counted.

Compare the reconstruction with both the previous template and a simple raw-source agent using the same model, corpus snapshot, task wording, and access. Match query budgets and report compilation and maintenance costs separately. Preserve actual prompts, tool access, outputs, evidence judgments, and timestamps. Measure correctness and evidence sufficiency before rewarding brevity or low token use.

Use human review of claim support and practical usefulness. Automated or LLM graders may help triage, but their disagreement and limitations should be visible. Keep development examples separate from held-out comparison questions; include unrelated guard questions after a repair. When the sample is small, report the individual outcomes instead of dressing them up as general performance estimates.

Useful outcome questions are: Did the owner gain a distinction or explanation they can apply? Could they verify the important conclusion efficiently? Did the system expose a consequential uncertainty? Could the next agent resume the work? Did a correction reach the dependent answer? Was the benefit worth the reading and maintenance effort?

**Revision condition:** add stronger retrieval when observed misses arise from discovery; add hierarchy when broad questions omit relevant regions; add finer claim structure when correction or attribution becomes unreliable. Simplify when metadata work repeatedly outweighs its demonstrated use.

## Settled / open

**Settled:** the strongest justified default is a small file-based compiler with evidence access, revision discipline, useful inquiry artifacts, and task-based evaluation. There are worthwhile additions beyond the gist, but no inspected source establishes a universally superior personal architecture. The recent direct papers provide research hypotheses and evaluation ideas; the established labs contribute narrower, well-motivated techniques.

**Open:** superiority over the previous template, gains in the owner's understanding, long-term maintenance cost, and the right retrieval scale remain empirical questions. Software checks and a successful demonstration cannot settle them.

**Strongest surviving objection:** even a well-designed compiled wiki can make inherited interpretations easier to reuse than to question, while shifting the owner's attention from difficult source material to fluent summaries. If repeated use reduces direct engagement and independent judgment, a simpler source collection plus deliberate inquiry may serve this purpose better.
